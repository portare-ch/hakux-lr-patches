name: 60 fps
description: Driving at 60 frames a second instead of 30, like the menus, at the game's normal speed. A frame the device doesn't finish in time shows a vblank late.
author: hakuX

[default.xbe]
# The game resets its display whenever it changes between the menus and
# driving (0xA39F0, called from its present at 0xAEEAB): the reset toggles
# its mode flag (0x39CDEC, 1 in the menus) and takes the presentation
# interval from it at 0xA3B4D: ONE with the flag set, TWO_OR_IMMEDIATE
# (0x80000002) without, so a driving frame waits two vblanks. The interval
# is ONE either way now: mov eax, 1 over the and / add that made it. The
# flag itself and what else reads it stay as they are.
# (Jao12345's 60 FPS patch in Xbox Magic Patches by Jay changes the same
# instruction, to ONE_OR_IMMEDIATE; this one was made without it.)
0x000A3B51: 25 FF FF FF 7F 05 02 00 00 80 -> B8 01 00 00 00 90 90 90 90 90
# The game steps by measured time (0x1890C0: the milliseconds since the last
# frame plus what the step before left over, in 1/3600 s), but by no less
# than a minimum (0x428EE8, set at 0x180AC1 from the 1/60 s at 0x3A4C60).
# Two vblanks never measure less; one vblank measures 16 or 17 ms, the 16 was
# raised to 16.667 and the 17 counted whole, and the game ran about 2% fast
# (its race clock 96.7 s in 94.6 s). The minimum is 1/120 s now.
0x003A4C60: 89 88 88 3C -> 89 88 08 3C
